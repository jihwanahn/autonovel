## Narrative Voice
The first-person voice is sardonic, self-deprecating, and hyper-rational — the protagonist deflects existential dread with technical problem-solving and dry wit. Sentences are short and punchy in the log entries; irony is weaponized constantly ('이 행성 최고의 식물학자'). Third-person scenes adopt a cinematic, procedural rhythm — cut-to-cut like a docudrama, building tension through jargon-laden real-time reporting. The tonal contrast between the intimate diary voice and the cold broadcast voice creates emotional whiplash that sustains engagement.

## Entertainment Formula
Five interlocking mechanisms: (1) Competence porn — the protagonist is genuinely expert, and watching expertise deployed under impossible constraints is intrinsically satisfying; (2) Situational irony — the absurdity of 'world's best botanist' being lectured by Earth botanists; cosmic stakes framed through mundane humor; (3) Mystery gap management — each log entry resolves one problem while opening two new technical unknowns; (4) Parallel montage catharsis — intercut between the isolated survivor's dry log and the collective anxiety of the watching world creates emotional amplification; (5) Competence-to-jeopardy cycle — the reader is allowed to feel confident in the protagonist's survival, then the equipment fails at the worst moment, resetting anxiety.
