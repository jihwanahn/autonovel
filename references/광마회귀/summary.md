## Narrative Voice
Dry, laconic, and deeply ironic interior monologue from a protagonist who performs nonchalance as armor. The rhythm alternates between terse, punchy self-commentary ('그런 편이지요') and sudden bursts of absurdist logic. The narrator editorializes constantly—self-deprecating yet quietly proud—and the voice is tonally flat precisely when events are most dramatic, creating comedic friction. Chapters are titled like episode headings or meme captions, functioning as micro-punchlines that reframe the chapter's content ironically before it even begins.

## Entertainment Formula
Four interlocking mechanisms: (1) Irony gap—the narrator's self-assessment is always slightly wrong in a way the reader can see, generating dramatic irony without tragedy. (2) Competence comedy—a protagonist who claims weakness or ignorance repeatedly demonstrates absurd excellence, so every disclaimer is a setup. (3) Mystery scaffolding—chapter titles function as teaser hooks; information about past-life events and unseen power hierarchies is rationed carefully, keeping the reader one revelation behind. (4) Warmth catharsis—moments of genuine human connection (a meal shared, a farewell, a child's candid complaint) are embedded in otherwise transactional scenes, releasing accumulated emotional tension as quiet satisfaction rather than melodrama.
